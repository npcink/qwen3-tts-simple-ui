# Authorized voice use policy

Use voice cloning only when at least one of the following is true:

- the speaker is you
- the speaker has given explicit permission for this specific use
- you have another documented legal basis accepted by the deployment owner

Do not use the service to impersonate a person, bypass identity checks, create deceptive endorsements, harass someone, or publish speech that a reasonable listener would mistake for an unauthorized real recording.

Deployment owners are responsible for access control, consent records, deletion requests, output retention and incident response. The UI consent checkbox is a workflow reminder, not identity verification or legal advice.
